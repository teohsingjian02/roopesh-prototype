AboutAbout

Ts. Dr. Roopesh Sitharan is a multidisciplinary artist, curator, researcher and academic based in Malaysia.

He is an Associate Professor at the Faculty of Creative Multimedia, Multimedia University (MMU), where he also leads the Master of Creative Multimedia programme. He is the Director of the Asia Pacific Artistic Research Network (APARN) from 2026, after convening it since 2024, and sits on the Board of Directors of the New Media Caucus.

He holds a PhD in Visual Culture from Goldsmiths, University of London, an MFA in New Media and Digital Arts from the University of California, Santa Cruz, and an MA in Curatorial Practice from California College of the Arts.
